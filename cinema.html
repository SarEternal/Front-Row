    Film finder

    Finds film titles and posters for you. It needs a free key from The Movie Database (TMDB).

    1. Make a free account at themoviedb.org.
    2. On a computer, open your account settings, choose the API link and request a key. It is a short form for a personal project.
    3. Copy the "API Key" (or the long "API Read Access Token") and paste it below.
    Film titles and posters

    The key stays on this phone. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
