Settings

Keys stay on this phone. TMDB supplies the film lists, Polish release dates and the audience score (TMDB user rating, not the Rotten Tomatoes audience score). OMDb supplies the Rotten Tomatoes critics score, IMDb and Metacritic. OMDb is often slow to get scores for new films; a free MDBList key fills those gaps and also gives the real Rotten Tomatoes audience score.
