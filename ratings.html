Scorecard

Polish cinema scores

Settings

Keys stay on this phone. TMDB supplies the film lists, Polish release dates and the audience score (TMDB user rating, not the Rotten Tomatoes audience score). OMDb supplies the Rotten Tomatoes critics score, IMDb and Metacritic.

Appearance

Not affiliated with Rotten Tomatoes, IMDb or Metacritic. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
